Website privacy

Your information.
Handled clearly.

What this website does with your information and what it doesn’t do.

The project brief tool

The form prepares text in your browser. It does not submit the contents to a server, store a copy in a database or save your entries in browser storage. Copying a brief places it on your device’s clipboard, which you control.

Opening an email draft passes the text to your chosen email application. The message is only sent when you choose to send it.

Hosting and technical requests

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it makes requests to GitHub’s infrastructure, which may process technical information such as your IP address and request logs. See the GitHub privacy statement for the hosting provider’s practices.

Cookies, fonts and analytics

No analytics, advertising scripts, embedded maps or tracking cookies are installed by Clearly Online on this website. Fonts and images are served with the website. Your browser may cache those files for performance.

Links to other websites

External websites and any email application you choose have their own privacy practices. This site does not control them.

Before enquiry collection is enabled

The studio’s contact details, retention approach and relevant privacy information will be completed before adding a server-side enquiry form or analytics. This page describes the current website functionality, not future services.
