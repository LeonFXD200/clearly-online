Websites for trades

Your work speaks for itself.
Your website should help.

A clear website for tradespeople and home service businesses across Sevenoaks and Kent. Show what you do, where you work and how a customer can request a quote.

THE JOB YOUR WEBSITE NEEDS TO DO

Less explaining.
Better enquiries.

A customer has a problem, a location and a phone in their hand. They need to know whether you handle that kind of job, cover their area and have a straightforward way to get in touch. A trades website should answer those questions quickly.

01

COMMON CHALLENGE

“Do you cover my postcode?”

Service areas are often buried or too vague. A clear coverage page and useful area information help people decide whether to enquire without claiming to serve places you do not.

02

COMMON CHALLENGE

“What exactly do you do?”

One long list makes different jobs hard to find. Separate pages for the main services can explain common work, any exclusions and what details help you give a price.

03

COMMON CHALLENGE

“Can I trust you with the job?”

Relevant photographs, accurate qualifications and a clear working process give people something real to judge. Any reviews or accreditations should be genuine and approved by your business.

04

COMMON CHALLENGE

“How do I ask for a quote?”

A mobile friendly form can ask for the job type, postcode, description and optional photographs if the agreed tool supports uploads. People can still call when the job is urgent.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What trade businesses
usually ask.

What should a trades website include?+

Start with your main services, actual coverage area, clear contact details and a mobile friendly way to request a quote. Add approved work examples and useful questions when they help customers choose you.

Can you use photos from my jobs?+

Yes, with your permission and any permission needed from the people or properties shown. We can plan how to label and organise them; original photography is not included in the website package.

Will a new website put me first on Google?+

No position can be promised. A technically sound site with useful service and location content gives search engines a clearer picture of your business; ongoing local visibility depends on more than the website alone.
