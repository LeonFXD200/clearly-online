GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions
you might have.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We can look at what's working, what needs improving and whether a focused redesign or a fresh build makes the most sense.

Do I need all my content ready?

No. We can start with what you have, plan the pages together and agree what copy, photos and business details are needed.

What happens after launch?

You can take a handover or choose hosting, maintenance and ongoing improvements. The support level and costs will be clear in your proposal.

Will I own my website?

For a one-off build, handover follows final payment. Ownership and transfer terms for a managed plan are set out in your written agreement before you commit.

Do you only work in Sevenoaks?

No. Good Measure is based near Sevenoaks and works with businesses around Tonbridge, Tunbridge Wells, the wider Kent area and beyond.
